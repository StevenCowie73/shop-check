Text messages from ColdenJames

ColdenJames texts one kind of person: someone who has just called a ColdenJames business number and not got through. This page shows every step of that, with the exact words used at each one.

  1. You find the number

    The number, (318) 666-6445, is published in two places, and each one says beside it what happens if your call is missed.

    On the homepage, coldenjames.com:

    Call or text (318) 666-6445. If I miss your call, you'll get one text back so you know I'll return it. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

    On letters ColdenJames mails to local trade businesses, printed under the number:

    If I miss your call, you'll get one text back. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

  2. You call, and hear this first

    When the call connects, before it rings through, you hear a recorded message:

    Thanks for calling ColdenJames. If I miss your call, I'll text you back at this number. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

    Staying on the line after this message is how you agree to get a text back. If you would rather not, hang up and nothing is sent.

  3. If the call isn't answered, you get one text

    It goes to the number you called from, and nowhere else:

    Hi, this is Steven at ColdenJames. Sorry I missed your call. I'll get back to you today, or just text me here. Reply STOP to opt out.

  4. If you reply, Steven gets it

    Replies are passed to Steven, who may answer you by text.

  5. How often

    One automated text per missed call, at most one per caller in any 24 hours, plus replies in conversations you start. Message and data rates may apply.

  6. Stopping, and getting help

    Reply STOP at any time and you will get no more texts. Reply HELP for help. These are the replies you get back:

    To STOP:

    You have successfully been unsubscribed. You will not receive any more messages from this number. Reply START to resubscribe.

    To HELP:

    Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Msg&Data Rates May Apply.

  7. What we never do

    Numbers are never taken from lists, bought, or shared for marketing, and ColdenJames sends no marketing texts. The only person who gets a text is someone who has just called.
